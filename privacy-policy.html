Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Privacy Terms Support

The short version. BuildUp has no accounts and no login. Your city, your best score and your achievements are stored on your own device. We never ask for your name, email address or precise location.

What does leave your device is limited to diagnostics and measurement: crash reports, performance and usage statistics, purchase records, and your IP address as an unavoidable part of any internet connection. Ad measurement only happens if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

This policy explains how Turnaworks (“we”, “us”) handles information in the BuildUp mobile game (“the app”). It applies to the app only, not to any other product or website.

Information we collect

Stays on your device

The following is written to your device's own storage and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your saved game, best score and unlocked achievements
  • Your settings — theme, sound, music, haptics, motion parallax

Deleting the app, or using Settings → Reset progress inside the game, removes this data.

Collected by the services we use

We use third-party software development kits (SDKs) that process data on our behalf or as independent controllers. Each is listed with what it handles and why.

IP address

Every service above receives your IP address, because it is part of how any internet connection works. It is used to derive approximate location at country level, to route responses back to you, and to protect against fraud and abuse. We do not use it to determine your precise location and we do not build a location history from it.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
  • Precise (GPS) location
  • Health, financial or biometric data
  • Payment card details — purchases are handled entirely by Apple, and we never see your card

Tracking and the iOS permission prompt

If we ask “Allow BuildUp to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites?”, that prompt is Apple's App Tracking Transparency dialog.

  • If you allow it, the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) may be shared with Meta so that advertising we pay for can be measured.
  • If you decline, the identifier is not shared and no cross-app tracking takes place. The game itself works exactly the same.

You can change your mind at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Why we are allowed to process this (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract — processing purchases so we can deliver what you paid for.
  • Legitimate interests — crash reports, performance data and aggregate usage statistics, so the game runs properly. We keep this to what is necessary and it is not used to build a profile of you.
  • Consent — advertising measurement based on the Advertising Identifier, which happens only after you allow tracking, and which you can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for money.

Data is shared only with the service providers named above, and only for the purposes named above. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, sharing an advertising identifier for ad measurement may count as “sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising”. Declining the tracking prompt, or turning tracking off in iOS Settings, opts you out of it entirely.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, safety, or the security of the app.

International transfers

Our providers operate globally and may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another approved mechanism.

How long it is kept

  • Crash and performance data: typically up to 90 days
  • Analytics events: up to 14 months
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to honour your purchase and to meet tax and accounting obligations
  • Data on your device: until you reset progress or delete the app

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR in the EU/UK, the KVKK in Türkiye, and the CCPA/CPRA in California — you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to receive a copy of it, and to complain to a supervisory authority. We do not discriminate against you for exercising any of them.

To exercise a right, email support@turnaworks.com. We will respond within 30 days. Because the app has no accounts, we may need information from you — such as your device's advertising identifier or a purchase receipt — to locate any data at all.

You can also act directly, without contacting us:

  • Settings → Reset progress inside the game erases your local progress and your Game Center achievements for this game.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking turns off ad tracking.

Children

BuildUp is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data in transit is protected with industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but our providers maintain their own security programmes and we keep the amount of data collected as small as the app can work with.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app or in the App Store release notes.
