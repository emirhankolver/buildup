BuildUp

From Shack to Skyscraper

A calm 3D puzzle game. Swipe to slide the board, merge two of the same building into the next one up, and grow a city from a lopsided shack to a landmark tower — eleven levels, no timers, no lives.

What's inside

  • Eleven buildings, read by silhouette rather than by number
  • Five places to build above — Canada, Switzerland, Egypt, Japan, Greece — each with its own landscape, palette and instrument
  • Music that is played rather than looped: a koto in Japan, an oud over a darbuka in Egypt, an alphorn in Switzerland
  • Game Center achievements and leaderboard
  • A power-saving theme that renders nothing at all
  • English and Turkish
